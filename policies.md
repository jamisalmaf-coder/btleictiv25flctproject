# Ethics, AI Use, and Output Ownership

Read together as a group before you build your pages. Your group page records your declarations.

## Responsible use of AI tools

Students may use AI tools to support learning, such as brainstorming, drafting, and reviewing, if AI use is declared, outputs reflect the student's own understanding and decisions, and all AI-supported content is reviewed, checked, and refined. Complete the AI declaration on your group page.

## Course Output Repository for Education (CORE)

All outputs in this course form part of CORE. Outputs are preserved and shared for academic purposes. Students keep authorship, and the institution ensures ethical use. Outputs may contribute to instruction, research, and program improvement.

## Professional accountability

All group members are collectively responsible for the quality and integrity of their work, ethical collaboration, and alignment with professional teaching standards, including the PPST and the TESDA Trainers Methodology competencies.

## Respect and safety of learners

No learner, guest, or participant may be embarrassed, pressured, put at risk, or have their privacy violated in the making of any output.

## Privacy rules for a class website {#privacy}

> This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Unless your school has an Enterprise plan, anyone with the address can open it. Treat every page as public.

- Use first names or initials only for classmates and guests. Do not post contact numbers, addresses, or private chats.
- Show a person in a photo only if they signed the consent form. Otherwise show the materials or the screen, or cover or crop the face.
- Do not upload video files to this repository. Link a private Google Drive video, shared only with the instructor.
- For the online session, record only with the permission of the platform host and the participants.
- Do not commit signed consent forms or raw photos of people you did not cover. Keep signed copies with your group and show them to the instructor on request.
- Delete raw photos, videos, and audio from personal devices and personal cloud storage after the compendium is graded and archived.
- Follow your institution's data privacy policy and the Data Privacy Act of 2012. Ask your instructor when in doubt.

## Participant consent for photos and video {#consent}

Sample wording. Adapt it to your institution's policy. Each group keeps its signed copies and shows them to the instructor on request.

Our group is a team of students taking Facilitating Learner-Centered Teaching with Emphasis on Trainers Methodology I (BTLE ICT IV-25). We facilitate a demo teaching session on an assigned topic, with classmates and invited guests as learners. We would like to take photos and videos of the session as evidence. Faces will not be shown on the class website unless you agree below. We use first names or initials only.

- ☐ I agree to take part in the demo session as a learner.
- ☐ Photos and video may be taken, with my face covered or not shown.
- ☐ Photos and video may show my face on the class website.

Fields to fill in on the paper or Google Form copy: participant first name or initials, section or affiliation, signature and date, and group member in charge. A participant may withdraw consent at any time by telling the group member in charge.

## Professional conduct and learner welfare pledge {#pledge}

As future trainers and teachers, we hold ourselves accountable for the dignity, safety, and privacy of every learner and guest connected to our work. We pledge that:

- No learner or guest was embarrassed, shamed, pressured, or put at risk during our sessions, and every participant was treated with respect.
- No violation of data privacy, child protection, or institutional policy was committed by any member of our group.
- Every photo or video of a participant was taken with their consent, and none of it was posted on public pages.
- We used first names or initials only, and did not post contact numbers, addresses, or private chats.
- We credit all images, music, stories, slides, and apps we use, and none were used in ways that violate their terms of use.
- If anything unsafe or harmful happens, we will stop the activity, make sure everyone is safe, and tell the instructor right away.
