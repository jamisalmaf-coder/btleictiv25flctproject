# BTLE ICT IV-25 Class Compendium

Facilitating Learner-Centered Teaching: The Learner-Centered Approaches with Emphasis on Trainers Methodology I

This class website takes the place of a printed compendium. Every group publishes a group page and a reflection page. Every member publishes an individual page with reflection answers, a TESDA sample session plan, and a recorded facilitation.

> **Keep personal details out of this site.** Use first names or initials for classmates and guests. Show a face only if that person signed the consent form on the [Policies page](policies/). Link videos from private Google Drive files. Do not post contact numbers, addresses, or private chats.

## How to edit your pages

1. Find your page in the repository, in the folder named after your group number (for example `g3`).
2. Click the file, then the pencil icon to edit.
3. Replace every `[note in brackets]` with your own work, and delete the note.
4. Scroll down, write a short message such as "Group 3 narrative report", and click **Commit changes**.
5. Wait a minute or two, then refresh the site to see the change.

The full student guide is in the repository's README.
